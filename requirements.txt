# The occlusion kernel (scripts/training_data/obstruction.py) and the smoke
# stats are numba-compiled.  Without it the AI panels lose their occlusion
# features and the players page's "穿烟" column degrades to "—"; the first call
# pays ~7 s of compilation.  It arrives with llvmlite (~150 MB) and caps
# numpy at <2.6.

# 逐帧遮挡每颗烟每个 journal 记录都要重建，少装它整条链路慢到不可用。
